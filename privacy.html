Privacy

What this website does with your information

Short version: almost nothing. Here is the longer version in plain language.

The short version

  • This website uses no cookies, no tracking, no analytics, no ads, and no accounts.
  • No third-party scripts, fonts, or widgets load on these pages.
  • Nothing you type into a form here is sent to, or stored by, this website.

When you contact me

The contact form and the free website check form do not send anything through this site. When you press the button, they open your own email app with a message already written, and you decide whether to send it.

If you email, call, or text me, the message reaches my business email (hosted by Google) or my business phone. I use what you share only to reply, to quote and do the work, and to keep ordinary business and tax records. I do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

The free website check

I look only at pages that are public on the address you send me. I do not log in to anything or look behind the scenes.

Hosting and visitor records

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical details of each visit, such as the visitor’s IP address, browser, and the pages requested. I do not receive or use that information. GitHub’s own privacy statement covers it.

Google

I use Google Search Console, which shows me totals such as how many times a page appeared in search results. I also have a Google Business Profile, so a listing for Sagebrush Studio LLC can appear on Google Search and Maps. Google’s privacy policy covers what Google collects.

Links to other sites

This site links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and Google, which have their own privacy practices. The four concept sites under Work are demonstrations for made-up businesses; they collect nothing, and their forms only open your email app too.

If you become a client

When we work together I will tell you what project files and content I keep, and where. If you ask me to, I will delete them once the project is finished.

Your choices

You can email me at any time to ask what I have about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. I usually reply within one to two business days.

Children

This site is for business owners and is not aimed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect information from children.
