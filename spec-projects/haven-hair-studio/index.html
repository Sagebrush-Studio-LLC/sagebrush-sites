About Haven

Haven Hair Studio was founded by Mara Whitfield, a stylist with over a decade behind the chair, after one too many clients asked for color that didn't need a touch-up every three weeks.

The studio is built around one idea: your hair should look intentional even on the days you didn't have time to style it. Every new client starts with a real consultation — not a rushed five minutes in the chair.
