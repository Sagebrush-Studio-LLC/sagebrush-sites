About Bloom

Started by a nail artist who refused to choose

Bloom Nail Studio was started by Jasmine Cole, who got tired of choosing between fast service and real artistry. The studio is built around clean, sanitary tools for every client, honest timelines, and a color wall big enough that you'll actually find "the one."
