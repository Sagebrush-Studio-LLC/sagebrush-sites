About Sagebrush Studio LLC

Sagebrush Studio LLC designs and builds websites for salons and beauty businesses — warm, mobile-first sites that load fast, show off the work through real photo galleries, and make booking an appointment effortless.

Sagebrush Studio LLC is founded by Michelle Feliciano, based in Fort Worth, with a background in computer information systems and over a decade of client-focused experience. Web development is new territory specifically, but working closely with clients and getting the details right isn't — no bloated templates, no slow page loads, just sites that look as good as the work they represent and get browsers to actually book. Every project gets the same attention to detail as the concept work you see in the Work section.
