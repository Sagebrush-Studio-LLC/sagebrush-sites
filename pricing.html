Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does a website take?

About 1½ weeks for a Starter site and 2½–3 weeks for a Salon site. The clock starts once I have your photos, services and prices, so the faster those arrive, the faster you launch.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo (if you have one), photos of your work and space, your services and prices, hours, address, and your booking link. No photos yet? Photo sourcing is available as an add-on, and so is copywriting if you’d rather not write the text.

Can I keep my booking platform?

Yes, and I’d recommend it. I link or embed whatever you already use, such as Vagaro, Square, Fresha, Booksy or GlossGenius, instead of replacing it.

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name, and you receive the site files at launch.

What does hosting cost?

Many of my sites can be hosted for free, so your ongoing cost is often just the domain name, typically $15–25 a year. If you’d rather use a paid host, I’ll set the site up there.

What happens after launch?

If something I built isn’t working, I’ll fix it at no charge. Content updates, such as new prices, photos or hours, are quoted per request, or ask me about a maintenance plan.

Will my site show up on Google?

I set up the search basics so Google can find and understand your site, and the SEO add-on goes further. Nobody can honestly promise a ranking, but a fast, mobile-friendly site with a free Google Business Profile gives you the best start.

Do you build online stores?

My focus is salon and beauty-business websites. If you sell products, I can link your site to the store you already use.

I already have a website. Can you improve it?

Start with a free website check. I’ll test its speed, mobile experience, search basics and accessibility, then send you a plain-English report, with no obligation.

Ready for a quote?

Tell me about your business and I’ll send a clear written quote, with no pressure.
